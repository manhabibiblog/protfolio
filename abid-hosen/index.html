About Me

Turning Ideas Into Digital Reality

A passionate freelance developer from Bangladesh, crafting world-class web solutions for clients across the globe.

I'm Abid Hosen, a professional Graphic Designer and UI/UX Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. With over 2 years of freelancing experience, I've helped startups, agencies, and enterprises build robust digital products that drive real results.

My journey began in 2018 when I left my corporate job to pursue freelancing full-time. Since then, I've completed 150+ projects for clients in 20+ countries including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and across Europe. I specialize in creating performant, accessible, and visually stunning web applications.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me mentoring aspiring Bangladeshi freelancers, contributing to open-source projects, or exploring the vibrant streets of Old Dhaka for inspiration. I believe in quality over quantity and treat every project as if it were my own business.
